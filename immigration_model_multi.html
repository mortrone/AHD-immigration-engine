Origin-Country Events

Model a war, famine, pogrom, land reform, or emigration policy in a specific source country. Shifts that country's share for a set window; the difference is drawn from (or redistributed to) every other tracked origin, proportionally. Works for origins already in the mix or brand-new ones (e.g. introduce "Polish" as its own tracked line after a partition-era crackdown).
